Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Application

This application is a personal Google Drive synchronization setup using Rclone, an open-source software application.

Google Account Data

Rclone accesses Google Drive data only to synchronize files and folders explicitly selected by the account owner.

Storage and Sharing

Synchronization is performed locally on the account owner's computers. Google Drive stores the synchronized files. No Google user data is sold, shared with advertisers, or disclosed to third parties by this personal setup.

Data Retention

Files are retained on the owner's computers and Google Drive according to the owner's synchronization settings and actions.

Contact

This is a personal application and is not offered as a public service.
